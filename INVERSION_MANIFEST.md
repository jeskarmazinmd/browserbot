# Persistent-loser inversion family

Paper-only prospective controls added for 17 previously persistent losers.

## New strategy IDs

- Cross-sectional: CSRANK5INV, CSRANK20INV, CSREV1INV, CSDISP1INV, CSBREADTH1INV, CSRELSPY1INV
- Event: EVTSEC8K1INV, EVTVOL1INV
- Options: OPTDIR1INV, OPTDIR2INV, OPTVERT1INV, OPTVERT2INV
- Strategy A checkpoint replay: K1INV, K2INV, K3INV, K5INV, K7INV

All worker strategies are paper-only. Originals are enabled beside inversions as prospective controls.
K inversions replay Strategy A as a short with symmetric target/stop distances and the original checkpoint rule.
